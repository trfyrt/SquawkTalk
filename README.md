**SquawkTalk** is a fast-paced, casual typing game where you take on the role of a bird trainer. Your goal? Teach your parrot as many words as possible in a high-intensity 60-second training session.

<sub>Inspired by competitive typing games like JKLM.fun</sub>

</div>

---

# 🦜 Key Features
- **Syllable-Based Prompts:** The parrot gives you a specific syllable (e.g., "TR"). You must quickly type a word containing it (e.g., "TRACK", "STRAY", "ULTRA").
- **The 60-Second Blitz:** A perfect "coffee-break" game. Short, addictive sessions that keep you coming back for a higher score.
- **Dynamic Combo System:** Elevate Chirpy’s mood by maintaining your typing streak! A **Hyped** <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trfyrt/SquawkTalk/master/Animations/ComboSprite/Hype/000.png" alt="Hype Chirpy" width="22px"> parrot doesn't just squawk—it skyrockets your score to new heights with massive multipliers.
- **Smart Parrot Ranking <sub>(soon!)</sub>:** At the end of each session, receive a fun "Intelligence Rank" for your parrot based on your performance, from "Sleepy Hatchling" to "Polyglot Professor."
